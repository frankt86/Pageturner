# Privacy Policy

Pageturner has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your books, your place in them, your bookmarks and your settings stay on
your phone.

Pageturner only goes online when you ask it to:

- **Covers from the internet:** when you search for a book's cover, the search goes to DuckDuckGo.
- **Audiobookshelf:** when you connect your own Audiobookshelf server, Pageturner talks to that server only.
- **Natural voice:** when you pick the natural read aloud voice, it's downloaded once from GitHub. Reading aloud then runs
  on your phone, and the text of your books never leaves it.

Questions? [Open an issue](https://github.com/frankt86/Pageturner/issues/new).
